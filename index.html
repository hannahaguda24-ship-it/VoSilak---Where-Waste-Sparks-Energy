System Information

How the Vosilak reward system works.

♻

Bottles Earn Minutes

Accepted plastic bottles add charging minutes to the selected port. Each accepted bottle earns three minutes of charging time.

⚡

Charge Your Device

Connect a device to any available port and start charging. The port counts down the remaining minutes in real time.

🎟

Unused Minutes Become Vouchers

When a charging session is finished early, any unused minutes automatically generate a unique voucher code.

🔁

Redeem to Recharge

Voucher codes can be redeemed to restore unused minutes to their original charging port for another session.

Note: Voucher minutes can only be returned to the port where the voucher was created. They cannot be transferred to another charging port.

How Vosilak Works

Turn plastic bottles into charging time in six simple steps.

  1. 1
    Insert a Bottle

    Select a port and click Insert Bottle. Follow the timer and click Done Inserting Bottle after your bottle is accepted.

  2. 2
    Earn Charging Time

    Every accepted bottle adds 3 minutes to the selected port.

  3. 3
    Start Charging

    Connect your device to the port and click Start Charging.

  4. 4
    Finish Charging

    Click Finish Charging when you're done. Unused minutes automatically generate a voucher code.

  5. 5
    Redeem Your Voucher

    Open the Voucher Redemption page and enter the generated code.

  6. 6
    Save for Another Recharge

    Confirm redemption to save unused minutes for another session on the original port.
